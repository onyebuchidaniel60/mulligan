Why not Pixabay or Mixkit
-------------------------
Pixabay refused automated access from this environment:
  GET https://pixabay.com/music/search/ambient%20tech/  -> 403
  GET https://cdn.pixabay.com/                             -> 403
Mixkit's pages load their catalogue through JavaScript. The tag page exposes five
preview URLs (assets.mixkit.co/music/<id>/<id>.mp3) but no track titles, artists or
licence metadata anywhere in the served HTML, and the per-track pages 404 on the
obvious URL shapes. A track whose licence and title cannot be cited fails the rule
"do not use a track whose license you cannot cite".

The Openverse API was used instead: it returns title, creator, licence and the
canonical source URL for each result, so the licence above is verifiable.
